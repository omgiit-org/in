NIELIT Digital Literacy Course

ACC

Awareness in Computer Concepts

20 Hours · Foundation Level Digital Literacy

Build essential awareness of computers and their everyday uses. This introductory course covers basic computer operations, document creation, internet access, email, financial literacy, online banking awareness and e-Governance services. It is suitable for beginners who want to become more confident using digital services.

OS-CIT

Odisha State-Certificate in Information Technology

3 Months · Basic Level

Learn the essential foundation of computer applications, internet usage, and practical digital skills. Ideal for students, job seekers, and beginners who want to build confidence in computer education from home. Students gain hands-on knowledge of MS Office, internet browsing, email usage, and basic digital tools needed for everyday work and government job applications.
