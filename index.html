Why OS-CIT

OS-CIT: Odisha State Certificate in Information Technology

It is a Government recognised basic computer proficiency course of OKCL. It is an international standard digital literacy course. It is a unique career-based computer application course providing excellent opportunity to the youths for learning digital literacy and career-oriented skills.

The OS-CIT learning programme builds practical digital skills through guided lessons and hands-on activities, including:

  • Introduction to computers and smartphones
  • Computer fundamentals and Windows
  • Internet, web browsing and email
  • MS Word, MS Excel and MS PowerPoint
  • Office 365, Google tools and mobile applications
  • Digital awareness, digital literacy and online safety
  • Basic coding and practical computer applications

The Odisha Gazette notification dated 19 March 2021 describes the OS-CIT qualification for consideration in relevant recruitment processes where basic computer proficiency is required. Candidates should always check the eligibility conditions in the specific recruitment advertisement.

The course is designed around digital literacy and practical IT skills, with learning areas aligned to commonly used digital productivity tools and international digital-skills frameworks.
